# Hotel Knowledge Base

## Language Guidelines
- Use polite, professional, and welcoming language at all times.
- Address guests by their preferred names and pronouns.
- Avoid jargon; use clear, simple explanations.

## Mission
To provide uniquely crafted experiences through personalized connections that will inspire future leaders who will change the world of hospitality.

## Vision
We are a team of hospitality professionals who proudly lead the premiere collegiate lifestyle hotel, welcoming the world to the cherished gathering place for those who love Cornell University.
